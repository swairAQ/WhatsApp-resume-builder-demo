How it works

Eight things his representative does

He does none of them. He answers yes or no.

01

One voice note, nothing typed

He talks the way he talks — Urdu, Punjabi, mid-shift, in traffic. The agent writes down role, area, experience, wage floor, availability and licence class.

02

A CV he never wrote

The profile becomes a clean one-page CV in English and Urdu — sent in the chat, shown at a gate, printed at the corner shop.

03

Matched against real work

Live openings ranked by distance, wage floor, shift and licence — not by keywords he does not know to use.

04

It asks before it acts

Nothing leaves his account without a yes. Consent is the product, not a setting buried inside it.

05

It speaks to the employer

With permission the agent introduces him, answers the first questions, confirms licence and start date — while he is still on the road.

06

The offer comes back better

It negotiates what a worker rarely can: wage, fuel, hours, start date. Ahmed asked for 40,000. The offer came back at 45,000 with fuel.

07

It keeps watching

A background watcher stays on his profile. When better work appears nearby it reaches him first — wage, distance and shift included.

08

A verified person, not a profile

Phone and identity are verified through Auth0 — employers know who is arriving on Monday, and no middleman can resell his details.

FAQ

Asked by workers, employers and investors

Does the worker pay anything?

Never. Placement is billed to the employer. A worker who is charged for access ends up representing the middleman instead of himself — that is the model we exist to replace.

What if he cannot read or write?

Then Kaamyaabi still works. The entire flow can be run by voice in Urdu or Punjabi — the agent speaks back, and every decision it needs from him is a yes or a no.

Can the agent message people without asking him?

No. Every outbound action is drafted, shown to him in his own language, and sent only on an explicit yes. Each message it sends in his name is written back into his chat.

How do you stop fake profiles?

Identity and phone are verified through Auth0 — one verified person, one profile, one number. An agent cannot list a worker he has never met, and a worker cannot be impersonated or resold.

Why WhatsApp and not an app?

Because he already has it, already trusts it, and it already handles voice. An install is a wall; a chat is a conversation he is having thirty times a day anyway.
